# morph-blend-lab — stochastic pattern blending under morph: the freeze exception and a lab prototype

- **Queue item:** T-007, the D-050 row (acceptance criterion 1: a lab prototype, heard by the human).
- **Why:** the human asked for stochastic blending in the morph between arp patterns and chose, by poll, to unfreeze it for 1.0 (D-050): every step, step patterns only, horde's corners driving the weights, the lab first. Today `pattern` switches like any discrete setting (SPEC §9, D-021), so the blend has to happen inside the core, at the step.
- **What changed:** `DECISIONS.md` D-050; the ROADMAP row with proposed acceptance criteria; `lab/arp-lab.html`: a `blend` input on the CORE (null by default, the spec's behaviour), `_blendPat` (draw lane 5, B with chance wB/1000), the step data and the tie look-ahead read from the drawn corner's pattern, the cycle length (repeats) still from the resolved pattern; on the morph card a "stochastic pattern blend" checkbox and a "load blend demo" button (A gallop, B octave leap).
- **Evidence consulted:** spec/SPEC.md §4.1, §4.3, §5.2, §9, §10; DECISIONS D-018, D-021, D-044; integrations/hypersaw/notice-preset-format.md:46 (four corners); lab/arp-lab.html CORE `_evalStep`, `_play`, and the morph adapter.
- **Provisional rules (each an open question for the design note, none ratified):** every corner's pattern keeps its own position (k mod its own length); the tie look-ahead uses the weight at the playing step, so a weight that moves before a looked-ahead step can play that step (the earlier note still ends at its scheduled off) or silence it; the cycle length for repeats is the resolved pattern's; lane 5 is new; two corners in the lab, four in horde.
- **Alternatives rejected:** a fourth Q-A1 rule in the rule menu (the blend covers the pattern only, while the rule menu governs every discrete setting, so the two are separate controls); resolving the blend in the adapter by flipping `pattern` per block (blocks are not steps: output would depend on the block size).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast`, exit 0 (lab smoke OK; the blend is off in every smoke session). A scratch headless check (not a gate) of the prototype, 24 of 24: at weight 0 or 1000 the output is byte-identical to pattern A or B alone; at 500 it differs from both, is deterministic, follows the seed, and is identical at block sizes 1, 7, 64, 512 and 4096; one terminal per note under a sweeping and a jumpy weight, for gallop/octave_leap, tied_legato/roll_end and three_three_two/tied_legato. In the browser the page loads with no console error and the demo button turns the blend on.
- **Open:** the human's listen; then the design note (`docs/design/morph-blend.md`) and its questions.
- **Follow-up (the human could not hear the blend):** the blend worked (the human's own session in the pane showed drawn octave leaps and a dropped step), but the demo pair was too alike: `gallop` and `octave_leap` agree on two of four steps, so a 50 % blend changed about one step in four. The demo is now `three_three_two` (A, sparse) against `roll_end` (B, every step, ratchets). The morph card shows which corner each of the last 16 steps came from (`corner` on the core's step log), and says that with morph on the Settings card does not drive the output. The draw's balance, measured over 100,000 steps at weights 100 to 900: B share within 0.002 of the weight, run counts as chance predicts. Verify exit 0; the scratch check 24 of 24.
