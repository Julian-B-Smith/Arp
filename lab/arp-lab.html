Chord stage before the arp

Each key you hold becomes itself plus up to six intervals, sent as its own chord stream (so horde can feed one oscillator the chord and another the arp). With the stage on, the arp plays the chord's notes. Untick arp on to hear the chord alone: the chord notes behave like held notes and keep sounding when you toggle the arp or stop the transport. The master enabled below switches the whole module.
Quantize (in Settings) snaps the chord too, including the note you play.

Randomize Arp's own, SPEC 15

The same function horde's Random panel will call, ported to the lab so the taste weights and bands can be tuned by ear. Structure draws the shape (order, pattern, rate, restart, repeats, velocity mode, scale, transposition, chord), Values the numbers (gate, swing, step time, velocities, randomness, seed), Both runs Structure then Values. Chaos is how far from home a draw may land (0 = home exactly); amount is how many of the keys are drawn. A press applies the result to the settings below like any edit. The same draw seed, click, corner, chaos, amount, locks and home give the same result every time.

Build your own

Your own step pattern (SPEC 4.6): pick user in the pattern setting to play it. pick says which held note a step plays: order takes the next note of the order style, 1-8 the k-th lowest held note, low / high the lowest / highest, chord every key. Only order steps advance the order, so an explicit pick leaves it where it was. Click a step's note / rest / tie button to cycle it.
a step of +1 moves the note by one step size: +1 at 12 semitones is an octave up.
