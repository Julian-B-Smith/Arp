Arp: listening sign-off

Every sound on this page was made by the real C++ core (the library that ships to horde), driven by a scripted host, then voiced by a small synth that is not part of the core. The arpeggio is a soft saw slightly to the left, the chord stage a rounder tone slightly to the right, and triggers are short clicks in the centre.

Per scenario: listen, look at the roll (the red line follows the audio; click the roll to jump), pick a verdict, and say what you heard in the box. Your answers are kept in this browser. When you are done, press Copy summary and paste it back to the lead.
